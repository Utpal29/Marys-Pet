What Mary does

Every groom is one-on-one, so your pet isn't stuck in a busy, noisy room.

Haircuts and styling

Breed trims, face and feet tidy-ups, and full haircuts for poodles, doodles, Shih Tzus and more.

Bath and brush-out

A proper wash, dry and brush so they come home clean and fluffy.

Deshedding

For double coats that shed everywhere, especially at the change of season.

Mat removal

Gentle work on tangled, matted coats for dogs and long-haired cats.

Nail trims

Quick trims for dogs and cats, including the ones that won't let you try at home.

First puppy grooms

A calm first visit so young pups learn grooming isn't scary.

Ear care

Ear cleaning and inner ear hair plucking.

Last-minute fixes

Home haircut gone wrong? Call and ask about same-day openings.

Questions

Don't see yours? Just call or text.

Do you groom cats?

Yes. Mary grooms cats as well as dogs, including nail trims and mat removal for long-haired cats.

My dog's coat is badly matted. Can you help?

Yes. Mary works on matted and double coats regularly. Call so she can hear about the coat before your visit.

My dog is nervous. Is this a good place for them?

Grooms are one-on-one, and reviews often mention how calm anxious and first-time pets are with Mary.

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Sometimes. Call and ask. Mary has fit in last-minute fixes before when her schedule allows.

How much does a groom cost?

It depends on size, coat and condition. Call or text with your pet's breed for a quote.
